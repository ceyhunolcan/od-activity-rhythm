# Figure Guide

This folder contains the main and supplementary figures for the manuscript:

**Olfactory dysfunction, daytime activity reduction, and 24-hour rhythm fragmentation - but not food-odor recognition - in NHANES 2013-2014**

## Main figures

| Figure | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Figure 1 | Cohort flow diagram and analytic sample construction |
| Figure 2 | 24-hour activity profile showing wake-period divergence between OD and non-OD adults |
| Figure 3 | Fully adjusted regression effects across primary activity and rhythm outcomes |
| Figure 4 | Age-stratified OD effects showing amplification in adults aged 60+ |
| Figure 5 | Minute-level fragmentation phenotype: ASTP/SATP symmetry, bout structure, prolonged sitting, and time-of-day decomposition |

## Recommended figure hierarchy

The main visual story should be:

1. **Figure 2:** OD adults diverge during waking hours, not sleep.
2. **Figure 5:** The deficit is not simple inactivity; it is a symmetric behavioral-fragmentation phenotype.
3. **Figure 4:** The phenotype is amplified in older adults.
4. **Figure 3:** The effects remain after full covariate adjustment.
5. **Figure 1:** Sample construction and exclusions.

## Flagship figure

Figure 5 should function as the manuscript's conceptual anchor because it captures the most novel contribution:

- active-to-sedentary transition probability increases
- sedentary-to-active transition probability decreases
- sedentary bouts lengthen
- active maxima shorten
- fragmentation is present across the wake period
- the pattern supports bidirectional behavioral rigidity rather than simple activity reduction

## Supplementary figure strategy

Supplementary figures should contain robustness and diagnostic analyses, including:

- hourly FDR contrasts
- food-odor falsification results
- sensitivity analyses
- IPSW / MICE robustness checks
- alternative cut-points
- OD-threshold sensitivity
- winsorization checks
